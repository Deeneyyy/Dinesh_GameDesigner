# Ting Senior Visualiser Portfolio: content and design notes

## Positioning

This page presents Dinesh as a visual designer applying for Ting's Senior Visualiser role. The focus is on translating briefs into clear visual communication, developing concepts, identity and campaign assets, product visualisation, thumbnail and promotional art, film/series visual support, and working with creative teams.

Game design stays in the experience section as an additional discipline. It is not the main headline or the central pitch.

## Ting design cues used

- Primary colour: Ting yellow `#ffee00` (the supplied source uses `#fe0`).
- Core palette: black `#000`, white `#fff`, soft grey `#f0f0f0`, warm grey `#f1efef`.
- Primary type: Satoshi.
- Secondary character type: American Typewriter / serif fallback. Bahary is retained as a decorative reference in the token notes, not used as a required font asset.
- Strong yellow bands, black sections, sharp contrast, large headlines, playful type accents, thin rules, grids, and occasional editorial labels.
- Responsive max-width reference: 1260px.

The site uses Ting-inspired design cues but does not reuse Ting's logo, proprietary copy, client case studies, testimonials or navigation.
