I like giving ideas
a visual language.

My background runs through Fine Arts, branding, graphic design, animation, UX, and game design. I like working out what an idea is really about, finding the visual thread, and making that thread work across formats.

That could be a logo, a campaign, a thumbnail, a motion piece, or an explanation of something technical. Sometimes AI-assisted tools are useful; often the answer is strong design fundamentals, careful art direction, and hands-on refinement. I care about making the result feel considered.

TOOLS, TECHNIQUE & ART DIRECTION

The tool is one part.
Craft makes it work.

My wider practice includes branding, graphic design, visual development, animation, and other hands-on creative work. Generative AI is one tool I use when it helps with exploration or storytelling; it does not replace design fundamentals. The important work is still research, composition, art direction, iteration, integration, and finishing.

I have also been working with Lights On Media Studios on integrating AI into their visual-effects workflow. I am interested in how new tools can support artists and existing production practices, and where they genuinely solve a creative or technical problem, while keeping human judgement and finishing at the centre.

Curious by default.
Particular about the craft.

I tend to go down rabbit holes. Sometimes that means pulling apart a visual system; sometimes it is figuring out how to host a service myself or understanding why a game mechanic works. I have often moved between subjects that do not look related at first, then found the thread that connects them.

I sometimes wonder whether I may be neurodivergent, though I have not been formally assessed and would not present that as a diagnosis. What I do know is that curiosity and hands-on experimentation are a big part of how I learn and work.

I am not claiming to be an expert in every discipline here. I know what I can bring today, and I am happy to learn from people who know more.
